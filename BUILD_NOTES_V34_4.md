# v34.4 — Interactive Budget Optimizer

- Adds personalized "Move this $X to [category]" actions for small DIY-friendly allocations.
- Destination is selected from the couple's #1 priority, other priorities, celebration preferences, then sensible core-category fallbacks.
- Applying a move sets the source allocation to $0 / DIY and adds the exact amount to the recommended destination while preserving the total budget.
- Each move supports Undo.
- Adds "Optimize My Budget" to apply all current DIY-friendly reallocations at once.
- Adds confirmation messaging after moves.
- Keeps the Portland DIY Wedding Guide as an alternate path and SEO/internal-link resource.
- Budget visualization uses the rebalanced allocations immediately.
