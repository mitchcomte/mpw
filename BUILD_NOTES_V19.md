# V19 — Wedding Builder Vendor Rosters

- Wedding Builder now creates three complete vendor rosters: Best Value, Our Best Match, and Dream Wedding.
- Couples can swap individual vendors and see the estimated roster total and budget cushion update instantly.
- One-click Contact My Roster uses the existing explicit-contact-consent lead flow.
- Matching now considers city/service area, style, priorities, guest capacity, ratings, category budget, and vendor-supplied pricing.
- Vendor dashboard now collects starting, typical, and upper pricing; guest capacity; wedding styles; and up to three named packages.
- Vendor-provided pricing is clearly presented as an estimate; final quotes and availability come from vendors.
- Production migration `wedding_builder_vendor_pricing_profiles` was applied to Supabase.
